Problem Statement

Our task is to design and prototype a washing machine that would be able to function in micro-gravity on spacecrafts. At the current moment astronauts are forced to use clothing until it gets so dirty that they are forced to get rid of it, which they do by sending into space to burn in the atmosphere. Our main goal with this design is to simulate washing a medium men's polo in the sink with water and wringing it out by hand.

Design Criteria

Size Requirements
Must fit within an ISS Single Locker
Vibration Limitation
No fast vibrations or fast spinning mechanisms
Water Usage
Water only (no soap of any kind) and a maximum of 1 Liter of water, ideally less
Fluid Management
Control all air and water within the microgravity environment
Agitation
Should be able to mechanically move the shirt around to agitate it without excessive bunching
Drying
Produce a damp shirt, not a soaking wet one
